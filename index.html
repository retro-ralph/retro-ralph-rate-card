Terms & Standards

How we work

Usage Rights

Default usage is organic reposting with credit. Paid media amplification, whitelisting, or extended licensing is priced separately. Scope, duration, and geography confirmed before work begins.

Exclusivity

Exclusivity windows are available and priced accordingly. Retro Ralph does not take on competing brands within a given category during an active partnership without prior disclosure.

Disclosure

All paid and affiliate content is clearly disclosed, in compliance with ASA guidelines and platform rules. Transparency is non-negotiable — it is also the reason this audience trusts what they read.

Red Lines

Retro Ralph does not work with fast fashion, hype-driven brands, or partnerships requiring scripted opinions or misrepresentation of quality. If it compromises taste or trust, it does not proceed.
