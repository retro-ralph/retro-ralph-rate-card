Advertising

We don't make ads

We make editorial that a brand is part of. It's why people watch to the end. That said, good work deserves to travel. If you want to put spend behind something we made together, here is how it works.

Partnership Ads

Partnership ads and whitelisting run from @retro__ralph, on pieces we made with you. Not on content made elsewhere.

Licence

90 days as standard. Longer on request.

Edits

Cutdowns and new captions are fine. We sign them off before they run.

Fee

A share of media spend, with a floor and a cap, settled in arrears. Or a flat licence fee. Agreed before launch.

Not On Offer

Scripted reads, product-first ads, or our voice on someone else's footage for paid media.

Disclosure

Paid and affiliate work is always labelled, in line with ASA guidelines and platform rules.

Terms & Standards

How we work

Usage Rights

Default usage is organic reposting with credit. Paid amplification is covered under Advertising, above. Scope, duration, and geography confirmed before work begins.

Exclusivity

Exclusivity windows are available and priced accordingly. Retro Ralph does not take on competing brands within a given category during an active partnership without prior disclosure.

Disclosure

All paid and affiliate content is clearly disclosed, in compliance with ASA guidelines and platform rules. Transparency is non-negotiable — it is also the reason this audience trusts what they read.

Red Lines

Retro Ralph does not work with fast fashion, hype-driven brands, or partnerships requiring scripted opinions or misrepresentation of quality. If it compromises taste or trust, it does not proceed.
